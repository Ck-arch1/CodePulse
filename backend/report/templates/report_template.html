Blast Radius Explanation

Blast radius estimates how many downstream call-graph connections may be affected by risky functions. Isolated unsafe patterns are downgraded unless taint, exposure, or high-confidence evidence increases contextual risk.

Limitations

This report uses lightweight heuristics for unsupported languages, Python AST traversal for Python, and non-symbolic runtime inference. Findings may include false positives, and unsupported languages do not receive deep parser-level analysis.

Complexity

  • Universal heuristics: approximately O(N)
  • AST traversal: approximately O(N)
  • Graph generation: approximately O(N + E)
  • Taint propagation worst case: approximately O(N^2)
  • Space complexity: approximately O(N + E)

N = AST nodes or tokens. E = graph edges.
